## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for mission-critical enterprise cybersecurity, regulatory intelligence, and autonomous AI compliance governance. It establishes an atmosphere of undeniable authority, calm precision, and institutional trust—counteracting the panic often associated with security incidents through a composed, naturalistic chromatic foundation drawn from deep forest waters and serene mineral tints.

The target demographic consists of Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs), risk officers, legal compliance directors, and AI model validators who analyze dense audit trails, cryptographic attestations, and compliance pipelines.

The aesthetic blends **Corporate / Modern architectural discipline** with nuanced **glass-etched tonal layers**. Clean structural hierarchy, high-legibility typographic scannability, hairline framing, and generous breathing room distinguish it from typical frantic red-and-neon dashboard aesthetics. Interfaces feel like a secure, high-end cryptographic operations hub: grounded, immutably verified, and serene under pressure.

## Colors

The chromatic language translates deep, calm botanical and mineral tones into high-precision technical interfaces:

- **Primary (`#162623`)**: Deep Obsidian Forest Teal. Used for primary typography, authoritative headers, key control states, and high-impact actions. It replaces harsh generic blacks with a dense, grounded pine-shadow depth.
- **Secondary (`#295255`)**: Slate Spruce Teal. Functions as the primary interactive accent, active navigation states, filter chips, and primary structural accents.
- **Tertiary (`#577877`)**: Sage Cyan Mineral. Employs soft, authoritative presence for secondary text, metadata labels, active border outlines, and subtle interactive hover foundations.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F0F5F7`)**: Pale Ice Mint / Soft Neutral. Serves as the overarching canvas background, providing gentle contrast without the harsh glare of stark white.

### Supporting Functional Accents
- **Crisp Surface Container**: `#FFFFFF` for data cards, elevated inspector panels, and modal dialogs.
- **Verification Emerald**: `#0E8A5E` (with soft fill `#E7F7F0` and border `#A7E4CD`) for cryptographic proofs, passing SOC 2 / ISO 27001 controls, and zero-trust verification tags.
- **Attention Amber**: `#C9770E` (with soft fill `#FDF6E8` and border `#F5D698`) for impending audit expirations, drift warnings, and pending approvals.
- **Critical Alert Rose**: `#C0323B` (with soft fill `#FCECEE` and border `#F5ACB2`) for security breaches, policy non-compliance, and model jailbreak threats.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric modernist balance of **Manrope** for primary narrative discourse and structural headings with the precision telemetry feel of **Space Grotesk** for metadata, audit signatures, keys, and tabular indicators.

### Usage Standards
- **Manrope**: Carries clear numeric geometry and open apertures, preserving immediate legibility across enterprise dashboards, compliance trees, and policy briefs.
- **Space Grotesk**: Reserved for numerical readouts, confidence intervals, badge labels, protocol IDs, hash previews, and status indicators. The subtle technical edge reinforces machine-verifiable integrity.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: For compliance scoring and percentage indicators, apply tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter across live telemetry updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy adheres to a disciplined **12-column fluid grid system** anchored within a maximum application viewport of 1600px, flanked by adaptive margins.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12-column grid with `2.5rem` outer page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates persistent left navigation (collapsible 260px/72px), multi-pane telemetry, and contextual side-drawer inspectors.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Split-pane structures collapse into stacked card arrays or tabbed subviews.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. Metric summaries condense to swipeable carousels; action buttons extend to full width.

### Rhythm
Components derive internal geometry strictly from the 4px/8px rhythm. Compact analytical density is prioritized in audit tables (`space-xs` to `space-sm` cell padding), while structural overview dashboards maintain generous outer whitespace (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to minimize cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows, establishing physical order through **luminescent ambient diffusion** combined with **subtle micro-borders**.

### Surface Levels
- **Canvas Base (`#F0F5F7`)**: Level 0 foundation.
- **Surface Crisp Card (`#FFFFFF`)**: Level 1 structural tier. Resting at pure white with a 1px perimeter border rendered in `rgba(41, 82, 85, 0.12)`. Supported by a gentle directional ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(22, 38, 35, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(22, 38, 35, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Interactive Card (Hover / Focused)**: Level 2 depth. Border shifts to `rgba(41, 82, 85, 0.28)` accompanied by elevated ambient lift: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(22, 38, 35, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(22, 38, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Overlays & Drawers**: Level 3 depth. Backdrops feature a translucent frosted tint: `rgba(22, 38, 35, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`. Floating modals feature a sharp 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` and `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(22, 38, 35, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The geometric form language communicates refined modernism:
- Standard interactive elements, input text boxes, and cards use an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius, balancing technical precision with tactile approachability.
- Containers, dashboards, and modal viewports deploy `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`).
- Pills and micro-tags (status badges, verification chips, security score pills) apply fully pill-shaped geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to distinctly isolate classification tags from structural card containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Forest Teal (`#162623`) solid background with pure white typography, 8px border radius, and `0.75rem 1.25rem` padding. On hover, background shifts to Slate Spruce (`#295255`). Focus rings produce an outer 3px glow in `rgba(41, 82, 85, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: White or transparent background with 1px solid border in `#295255`, secondary text color (`#295255`). On hover, acquires a subtle mint wash (`#E6EEF0`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Subtle minimal hover states, no rest border, text in `#295255`.

### Chips & Verification Badges
- Displayed with fully rounded pill contours and uppercase or small-caps `Space Grotesk` typography (`label-sm`).
- **Verified / Compliant**: Light emerald fill (`#E7F7F0`), 1px emerald border (`#A7E4CD`), text `#0E8A5E`, prepended by a crisp check-shield SVG icon.
- **In Review / Warning**: Amber fill (`#FDF6E8`), amber border (`#F5D698`), text `#C9770E`.
- **Non-Compliant / Threat**: Muted rose fill (`#FCECEE`), rose border (`#F5ACB2`), text `#C0323B`.
- **Standard Filter Chip**: Pale neutral background (`#FFFFFF`), border `rgba(41, 82, 85, 0.15)`, text `#295255`. On active selection: solid `#295255` with white text.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) body, 16px corner radius, framed by a soft 1px border (`rgba(41, 82, 85, 0.10)`).
- Card headers maintain clean baseline alignment with `label-lg` or `headline-sm`, separated from body data by hairline dividers (`#E4ECEE`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs**: Crisp white background, 8px corner radius, 1px perimeter border in `#577877` at 30% opacity. Height fixed at 40px for desktop workflows. Active focus prompts a 1px border in `#295255` and a 2px outer aura in `rgba(41, 82, 85, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px dimensions, 4px border radius for checkboxes and 50% for radios. Unchecked border `#577877` (50% opacity). Checked state fills `#162623` with a crisp white checkmark or centered node.

### Lists & Audit Tables
- Alternating rows avoid heavy zebra patterns; instead, employ pure white rows with a subtle hover transition to `#F7FAFA`.
- Hairline horizontal dividers (`#E8EFEF`).
- Column headers utilize `Space Grotesk` (`label-md`), rendered in uppercase with `#577877` and subtle sorting chevrons.

### Iconography Style
- Rendered in a clean, geometric 1.5px monoline stroke using rounded terminal caps.
- Core metaphors center on cryptographic proof, hardware security tokens, structural locks, policy nodes, and shield verifications.
